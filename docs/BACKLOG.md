### Chapter admins cannot view CVs (access rights)
As a chapter (local) admin, participant CVs are not visible. Suspected cause: access-rights / permission scoping for chapter admins does not grant read access to CV files (Google Drive links and/or the guard on the CV-serving path). Expected: a chapter admin should be able to view the CVs of applicants/participants within their own chapter, just like a global admin can within scope.
